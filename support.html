Getting a good result

How should I say a job?

Talk the way you'd tell a mate. Say who it's for, what you did, and what it costs: "Invoice for Sarah Mitchell. Kitchen sink repair, parts eighty five dollars, labour two hours at ninety five an hour."

Saying "at" before an hourly rate ("two hours at ninety five") removes most ambiguity — without it, Talkbill often has to ask whether you meant two items or one.

I said the wrong number. Can I correct myself out loud?

Yes. Say "actually, make that three hours" or "no wait, eighty dollars" and Talkbill changes the line instead of adding a new one. If it can't tell what you meant to change, it asks rather than guessing.

Can I do several jobs in one go?

Yes. Start each one with "Invoice for <name>" and Talkbill splits the recording into separate invoices for separate customers.

Why is a line marked "Needs review"?

Because Talkbill wasn't certain, and it will not quietly send an invoice it isn't sure about. "Two smoke alarms thirty five dollars" could be 2 × $35 or a single $35 — so it shows both and you tap the right one. A document with an open question can't be sent until you answer it.

Problems

It isn't hearing me

Check that Talkbill has microphone access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. On a noisy site, hold the phone closer and speak in short sentences — pause briefly between line items.

"Language pack not ready"

iOS downloads the on-device speech pack once, over Wi-Fi, and keeps it. Connect to Wi-Fi and try again. Once it's installed, Talkbill works with no signal at all.

The invoice came out wrong

On a draft, scroll to the bottom and tap Copy debug report, then paste it into an email to us. It contains exactly what was heard and what was extracted — that's far more useful than a screenshot, and it usually becomes a permanent fix rather than a one-off answer.

My invoices aren't on my other device

Open Settings inside Talkbill and check the iCloud sync line. If it says Off, sign in to iCloud on the device. Talkbill works fine without it — everything simply stays on that one phone.

Billing

What's free?

Your first invoice — and it stays yours forever. Reading your archive, editing, marking invoices paid, and exporting to PDF or CSV are never locked, subscription or not.

How do I cancel?

iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Talkbill → Cancel. One tap, no email to write, nothing to ask us for. Cancelling never deletes your invoices.

I bought Lifetime and it's not showing

Open the paywall and tap Restore, signed in to the same Apple account you bought with. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

What Talkbill doesn't do

Talkbill writes invoices and tracks what you're owed. It does not do your taxes, doesn't connect to your bank, and doesn't give financial advice. Any tax or discount percentage on an invoice is the one you said out loud.
