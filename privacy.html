Privacy Policy

Talkbill · Last updated 12 November 2026

Your invoices never leave your phone. Talkbill has no account system, and there is no database of ours that your invoices could sit in. What you bill, who you bill, and what you said out loud stay on your device.

What happens to your voice

When you describe a job, the audio is transcribed on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The recording is not saved and the audio never leaves your device. Talkbill does not upload audio or transcripts anywhere, and does not use any cloud speech service.

Where your invoices live

Your customers, invoices and business details are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, iOS mirrors them to your own private iCloud database so they survive a lost or replaced phone. That storage belongs to your Apple account — we have no access to it, and we cannot read, export or delete anything in it. You can turn iCloud off in iOS Settings; Talkbill keeps working entirely on the device.

Third parties

Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store. We never see your card or payment details. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active, and when you buy, renew, cancel or get a refund, Apple also sends a purchase notice to Superwall, the service we use to keep track of subscriptions. That notice says which plan, when, the price and App Store country, Apple's reference number for the purchase, and a random install number. It never includes your name, email or card.

The install number is created at random on your phone and sent only with a purchase, so that a renewal can be matched to the purchase it belongs to. It is not your name, your device ID or an advertising ID, and it is not shared between your devices. Neither the purchase notice nor the install number is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you write to us

The support page has a contact form. If you use it, we receive what you send: your email address, your message, the topic you picked and any screenshot you attach. The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which passes it to our support inbox (Gmail) and keeps its own copy for up to 30 days. We use your email only to answer you — never for marketing — and it is not linked to your purchases or your install number. Ask us through the same form and we will delete your messages.

Permissions

Microphone — used when you speak an invoice, and only while you are actively dictating. You can type invoices instead without granting it. Photos — optional, only if you pick a logo for your invoices; the image is stored with your business profile on your device.

Your data is never held hostage

If your subscription lapses, your invoices remain readable and exportable — as CSV and PDF — for as long as the app is installed. Ending a subscription locks sending new invoices, never your existing records. Deleting the app removes the local data; anything mirrored to iCloud is removed through your Apple account.

Children

Talkbill is a tool for running a business and is not directed at children. We do not ask anyone — of any age — for personal details: the app sends us only the purchase record and install number described above, and neither includes a name, age or contact details. If you write to us, we also have the message you sent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above changes with it. The table above is everything the app collects today, so any addition to it would mean a change in the app itself — and that would be described in the App Store release notes too.
