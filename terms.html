What Talkbill does

Talkbill turns what you say into a draft invoice or estimate on your iPhone. It transcribes your speech on-device, extracts the customer, the work and the amounts, and produces a PDF you can send. Everything runs on your phone.

Your responsibility: read it before you send it

Speech recognition is not perfect. Numbers are the hardest part of it: "eighty" and "eighteen" sound alike, and "eight fifty" can mean 8.50 or 850. Talkbill is built to notice when it is unsure and ask you rather than guess — a document with an open question cannot be sent until you answer it. That safeguard reduces mistakes; it cannot remove them.

So the rule is simple, and it is the most important line on this page: you are responsible for checking every invoice before you send it. Once you send it, it is your document and your figures.
